## Worker or D22
**Worker (D18), built and working; D22 was not needed.** `new Worker(new URL("./engine.worker.ts", import.meta.url), { type: "module" })`
compiles under Turbopack (Next 16.3.8) on the first attempt: the worker gets its own chunk, the IronCalc glue is a lazy chunk the worker
loads, and the wasm is started from `/vendor/ironcalc-0.8.4.wasm` (`wasm.default({ module_or_path })`). The e2e proves it end to end in
Chromium at 375 and 1280 (the scenario output equals the fixture script's arithmetic). The spec was not changed.
Side note: Turbopack also emits a raw copy of `engine.worker.ts` under `.next/static/media/` (an asset for the `new URL`). It holds no
secret (the same code is in the chunk); it is not served from any other route's chunks (the bundle check walks them).
