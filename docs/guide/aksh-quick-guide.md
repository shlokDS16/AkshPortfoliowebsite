# The desk: quick guide

## 1. What the desk is for

The desk is your private research system, behind sign-in. You note thoughts, keep a file on each company, read company documents with help, and record your ideas and how they turned out. The public side is a showcase anyone can read. Nothing goes there by itself: a company file goes public only if you press publish and it passes the checks. Your captures, the Inbox and the whole Ledger stay private.

## 2. Signing in

There is no password. You sign in with a link sent to your email.

1. Open the site's sign-in page, type your email in the **Email** box and press **Send sign-in link**.
2. The page says: "If this address is allowed, a sign-in link is on its way. Check your inbox." It says this for every address, on purpose.
3. Open the email and press the link.

The link only works in the browser that asked for it. If you ask on your laptop and open the email on your phone, it will not work. Ask again and open it where you asked.

If the link does not arrive: check spam, check you typed the address the site knows, wait a minute or two and ask again. If it still does not arrive, tell Shlok. To leave, press **Sign out** in the top bar.

## 3. Your daily routine (5 minutes)

1. Open the desk. A red strip at the top means something is late or broken (section 6).
2. Capture what you noticed: one line on the **Capture** tab, then Enter. Do this whenever a thought comes.
3. Clear the "Needs you" box on the Capture tab.
4. Check the **Inbox**. If a document says "Ready", press **Review**.
5. Open the **Ledger** tab and read its "Needs you" box. If your thinking on an open idea changed, add a note.
6. If you closed an idea, write the lesson while it is fresh.

On a quiet day, steps 1 and 2 are enough.

## 4. How to...

### Capture a thought

1. Open the **Capture** tab. The box is already selected.
2. Type your thought. Optional: `$RELIANCE` links it to a company, `#capital-cycle` links it to a concept (you can write several `#names`; the first 8 are linked).
3. Press **Enter** to save. (**Shift+Enter** starts a new line.)
4. Under the box you will see "Saved." If you are offline it says "Saved on this device, will sync (N waiting)." Nothing is lost.

A `$SYMBOL` or `#concept` the site has not seen goes to the **Names** tab, where you choose **Same as ...**, **New company** (then **Yes, add it**), or **Not a company, keep as text**.

Every `#name` you write becomes a concept, with its own page under **Concepts** on the **Items** tab that gathers your notes on it. Start a capture with `r:` to log something you read; it stays private. Press **This month** on the Items tab to see what you learned.

### Start a company file

1. On the **Capture** tab, type for example `$KAVERI t: I expect margins to hold because ...`
2. Press **Enter**. `t:` at the very start means a thesis (your view on a company). With a `$SYMBOL` it starts that company's file, or adds a new revision if one exists.
3. The other way: on a document's review screen, press **Start a file for ...** (a private, empty file).
4. Find the file on the **Items** tab.

### Add a document and check its figures

1. Open the **Inbox** tab.
2. Press **Choose a file** (a PDF up to 50 MB, or a photo of a table). For a link or text, use **Or paste a link or some text** and press **Read it** (text needs at least 50 characters).
3. Keep the Inbox tab open while it reads.
4. When the card says "N figures ready to check", press **Review**.
5. Flagged figures come first, one at a time ("Check 1 of 3"). Read "The page line", then either type the value from the page (**Value as printed on the page**) or choose **Drop it**.
6. Under "All checked", untick any figure you do not want. At the bottom, under **File under**, fill in **Document title**, **Type** and **Filed on**, then press **File N figures under <file title>**.
7. The figures wait in that file's facts form. They are not part of the file until you save a revision there.

You decide each figure. Check scanned figures with extra care.

### Publish a company file

1. In the file's **Details**, fill in **Learning objective**, **Holds position** and **Figures to** (the date your newest figure runs to). Press **Save details**.
2. Read the **Publish checklist**. If it shows "Company is public" failing, press **Make <company> public**.
3. Tick "I have not changed my stance on <company> in my private notes in the last 30 days." only if that is true.
4. Press **Run the publishing gate on revision #N**.
5. If it says "Blocked by the gate", the flagged sentence is highlighted. Press **Edit sentence**, save a new revision, and run the gate again. If it passes, the file shows on the public site once its Figures to date is 30 days old.

### Record an idea in the Ledger

1. On the **Ledger** tab, press **Record an idea**.
2. Choose the **Company**. A greyed-out company needs settling on the **Names** tab first.
3. Under **What do you expect?**, choose "Expects to do better than the Nifty 50" or "Expects to do worse than the Nifty 50".
4. Choose the horizon (6 months, 1 year, 3 years, or **A number of days**) and answer **Do you hold it?**
5. Write your reasons in **Why do you think so, and what would change your mind?**
6. Press **Record the idea**. You see "Idea recorded and sealed."

The entry price is set at the next close; the idea has no score until then.

### Add an entry to an idea

1. Open the idea and go to **Add to this idea**.
2. Under **What do you want to add?**, choose one: **Add a note**, **Revise the thesis or the horizon**, **Record that I bought more**, or **Record that I sold some**.
3. Write your words in the box and press the button (for a note, **Add the note**). You see "Entry added and sealed."

### Close an idea and write the lesson

1. On the idea, choose **Close this idea**.
2. Choose a **Reason**, write **Why you are closing it**, and press **Close the idea**. The exit is set at the next close. You cannot reopen it.
3. Choose **Write what I learned**. Fill **What you learned**, choose **Was the process sound?**, and tick at least one **What kind of mistake, if any?**
4. Press **Save what I learned**. Your lessons are listed under **What I learned** on the Ledger page.

### Write up a closed idea as a case study

1. Close the idea and write the lesson. Open the idea: in the **Case study** block, press **Draft a case study** once the exit price is fixed.
2. On its page, fill in **Learning objective** and **Holds position**, add your "Looking back" words if you want to (no price and no return in them), and run the publishing gate. It goes public only if Shlok has switched case studies on, and only 30 days after the latest entry it shows. Your entries cannot be edited there; if you write a new lesson, press **Refresh from the ledger**.

### Save a source (someone else's video or article)

1. In the Capture box type `s:`, paste the link, add `$SYMBOL` or `#concept` if you like, and write what you think. Enter. The receipt says "Source, private". Or use **Items**, then **Sources**, then **Add a source**.
2. Save the same link again and it is still one card: your new note is added under a dated line. Nothing is read twice.
3. The desk reads the title and the picture once and shows them on the card in the machine's type, apart from your note.
4. To show it on a company file: open the file, find **Outside sources**, tick **Show on the public file**, press **Save as a new revision**, then run the publishing gate. It is on the public file as soon as the gate passes; there is no 30-day wait for these.
5. **Show its title and picture** is ticked by default. The desk does not read pictures, so untick it if the picture tells people to buy or sell. Untick it too if the gate refuses the title (a title with a price or a return in it, or an instruction to buy or sell, is refused because the title is someone else's sentence and you cannot change it). The link alone then passes.

### Add a video

Videos and letters are switched off for now (the code has them off), so the Videos and Letters links are not on the desk. The steps below are for when Shlok turns them back on. They also need a YouTube key. The desk then shows "Video listing is not set up yet (Shlok adds a YouTube key)." You can do the steps in order once it is on.

1. Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted**. Keep it Unlisted until the desk lists it.
2. In the Capture box type `v:` and paste the link, then Enter. Open **Items**, then **Videos**, then the video. Press **Fetch again** if it says "Waiting for YouTube".
3. Get the caption file: YouTube Studio, **Content**, the video, **Subtitles**, the three dots on the language row, **Download**, **.vtt** or **.srt**. If an automatic caption has a wrong word, fix it in Studio first and download again.
4. On the video page, drop the file under **Transcript** and press **Store transcript**, then **Use this transcript**. Press **Use chapters from the description** (at least 3 lines that start with a time, the first at 0:00). Write the **Learning objective**.
5. Run the publishing gate. Then, and only then, make the video Public on YouTube.

What the desk refuses, and why: the transcript is what a visitor reads, so the same words are refused as in a note. A spoken "you should buy this" is blocked under "Rule 1: no actionable language" and you cannot edit the transcript to hide it. If the sentence is you explaining your process, allow that one sentence with a reason; otherwise say it differently in a new video. It also refuses a video that is not on your channel, is private, was last read from YouTube more than 7 days ago, or names a company with figures less than 30 days old. A spoken claim about your returns has no allowance.

### Send a letter

Letters are switched off for now. When they are back on, letters are email, and nothing is emailed until Shlok has set up the email service; until then the desk says "Email is not set up yet. Letters still publish to the archive." Readers ask for letters on the site's letters page (they tick that they are 18 or older). It never emails anyone by itself. You make two presses.

1. **Items**, **Letters**, **Readers**: press **Send confirmation emails (n)**. Each person gets one email with a link, and they press a button to confirm. A reader may wait up to a day. **Bin** deletes a request you do not want.
2. **Items**, **Letters**, **What changed**: untick what you do not want, press **Make this letter**, replace the "Untitled letter" subject with your own (**Send** stays off until you do), write the one sentence, **Save**, read the preview, run the publishing gate. Then press **Send to n subscribers**, read the box (it shows the exact number), tick "I have read the preview" and press **Send**. An email cannot be called back, and a second press sends nothing more.

If a reader asks you to delete their address, use **Erase an address** on the Readers page and reply that it is deleted. Readers can stop any time with the link in every email.

### Save a public page as a PDF

Open the public file, note or closed case and press **Save as PDF** under its dates. In the print window, choose "Save as PDF" as the printer. The PDF says which revision it shows and carries the full disclosure. A copy already saved cannot be called back if you change or unpublish the page later. The feed and the shared-link cards follow what you publish within an hour.

### Add prices when they are missing

The desk fetches closing prices by itself each morning. Use the **Closes and corporate actions** page (from the Ledger tab) only when something is missing.

1. First press **Fetch closes now**. Read the result lines.
2. If NSE does not answer, download that day's files from NSE. Under **Drop NSE's official file**, fill in **Trading date of the files**, **Equity file (the day's bhavcopy, a .zip)** and **Index file (the day's indices, a .csv), optional**, then press **Store the official closes**. Drop the index file too: the Nifty 50 close opens the day.
3. For one company on one day, use **Type a close**: choose **Company**, **Trading date** and **Closing price of the company**, then press **Store the close**. Not before 15:30 India time, and not on a weekend.

### Record a split or bonus

1. On the Closes page, go to **Splits, bonuses and other actions**.
2. Choose the **Company** and **What happened** (Split, Bonus, Other, or No action needed).
3. Enter the **Ex-date** and the **Factor (0.001 to 1000)**: enter 5 for a 1:5 split, 2 for a 1:1 bonus. For "No action needed", leave it at 1.
4. Press **Enter the action**. You see "Action entered. It is marked on every score whose period it falls in."
5. To change one, press **Correct this entry** on it.

## 5. Rules worth knowing

- **Nothing that reads as advice.** You are not a SEBI-registered Research Analyst, so the site refuses words like "buy", "sell", "target price" and "undervalued by X%" about a named company. Write what you expected and why.
- **No performance claims on the public side.** Phrases like "returned X%" or "hit rate" are refused.
- **The 30-day wait.** Figures about a company must be at least 30 days old. Set **Figures to** to your newest figure's date. The file goes public once that date is 30 days old.
- **After you open, close or resize an idea on a company, any file about that company waits 30 days.** Notes, revisions and lessons do not count. The checklist says "Rule 4: clear from <date>". If you plan to publish a file on a company, publish it before you record an idea on it, or accept the wait.
- **The position check.** If the Ledger shows you hold an open position in a company, **Holds position** cannot say No: "The ledger shows a position in this company; the disclosure cannot say no."
- **Ledger entries cannot be edited or deleted from the screens, and the desk sets every date.** To change your mind, add a new entry.
- **You can withdraw a new idea only for a short time** (at most 24 hours; the form shows the exact time). After that you can only close it.
- **Record NO test ideas on the live site.** An entry can never be removed. Even a withdrawn test idea stays on the screen for good.
- **Your first idea may wait up to three days for its entry price.** **Fetch closes now** on the Closes page brings it forward (press it up to three times and read the result line).
- **The consistency check.** The Ledger page says "Consistent with itself: N entries, M sealed days." That means the entries and the sealed days agree with each other. It is not an outside audit.

## 6. If something looks wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Red strip: "Background jobs are late: the daily job last ran 2 days ago. Your notes are safe; documents wait until the jobs run." | Wait a little and reload. If it stays, tell Shlok. |
| Red strip: "Background jobs are late: the closing prices' last run failed ..." | Scores may be a day behind. On the Closes page press **Fetch closes now**. If it still fails, drop NSE's file and tell Shlok. |
| Ledger page, red box: **The ledger failed its own check** | Do not try to fix it. Tell Shlok. You can still record ideas and entries. If it says "Could not check", reload; if it repeats, tell Shlok. |
| "Needs a check: the price moved sharply. Was there a split or bonus?" | Enter the split or bonus on the Closes page, or choose "No action needed". |
| Publish refused: "Publish stopped" card in "Needs you" | Press **Open the gate notes**, fix the flagged sentences, run the gate again. |
| A document card says "Today's free AI allowance is used up ..." or "Pages 142-147 could not be read." | First: wait. Second: press **Try again**, **Skip** or **Enter manually**. |

For anything else, copy the exact words on screen and send them to Shlok.

## 7. Not in this version

A public view of the Ledger or any figure for how your ideas did (a closed idea can be written up as a case study, once Shlok switches that on); the spreadsheet valuation page; a letter written for you or sent on a schedule (letters go out only when you press); a PDF made on the site's server (Save as PDF uses your browser's print window); logins for readers; voice notes; editing or removing a split or bonus (correct it with a new entry).
