Segment 1, round 2: Labelled Blocks, improvised and hybrid

Three frames render the same fictional Kaveri Pumps thesis from one shared data object. B is round 1, unchanged, for reference. B+ pushes the research-file idea further. H is B+ markup set in direction A's type. Company, ticker, figures and quoted filing lines are invented to test layout.

Colour is neutral greys on purpose (identity is segment 5). Judge structure, type, numbers and states. In B+ and H, tap a source chip such as S1 · p. 112 to open the line it came from, open the strip at the top for the disclosure summary, and switch the history between changes, R2 and R1. "Empty states" swaps in a first draft that has one version and no sources.
